# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

RtG-Format is a community research and software project. We want participation to be respectful, constructive, and focused on improving technical accuracy and project quality.

## Expected behavior

Participants are expected to:

- Be respectful and considerate, including during technical disagreement.
- Critique ideas, evidence, and implementations rather than attacking people.
- Provide useful context when reporting issues or proposing changes.
- Accept good-faith corrections and acknowledge uncertainty.
- Respect contributors' privacy and avoid sharing personal or sensitive information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory conduct, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, and repeated disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer through the contact method listed on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Include links or context where possible, but do not publish private information. The maintainer will review reports as time and availability permit and may remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary.

This policy is intended to support constructive collaboration; it does not guarantee a particular response time.
